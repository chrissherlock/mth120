Podcast Timecode Logger

Map the start and end times for concepts discussed in your podcast.

Choose a local audio file to begin.

Streamlined Workflow:
• The segment automatically Starts from 0:00 (or the end of your last segment).
• Press E to mark the End of the current concept. The audio will pause.
• Type the description, then press Enter. The audio will instantly resume and start a new segment automatically.
