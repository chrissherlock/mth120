Module 1: Punctured Neighborhoods & Formal Definitions

Question 1: The Punctured Neighborhood Constraint

What is the primary mathematical purpose of the inequality $0 < |x - c| < \delta$ in the formal definition of a limit, compared to the continuity condition $|x - c| < \delta$?

Think about what happens when $x = c$ causes $\frac{0}{0}$. Why do we want to exclude $x = c$ from our test window?

Module 2: Algebraic Surgery & Factoring ($\frac{0}{0}$)

Question 5: Basic Polynomial Factoring Limit

Evaluate the limit: $$\lim_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2 - 16}{x - 4}$$

Factor the numerator as a difference of squares: $x^2 - 16 = (x - 4)(x + 4)$. Then cancel $(x - 4)$.

Question 6: Quadratic Factoring

Evaluate the limit: $$\lim_{x \to -2} \frac{x^2 + 5x + 6}{x + 2}$$

Factor the numerator $x^2 + 5x + 6$ into $(x + 2)(x + 3)$.

Question 7: Difference of Cubes

Evaluate the limit: $$\lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^3 - 8}{x - 2}$$

Recall the difference of cubes formula: $a^3 - b^3 = (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2)$. Here $a = x, b = 2$.

Question 8: Non-Zero over Zero Asymptote

Evaluate the limit: $$\lim_{x \to 3^+} \frac{x + 5}{x - 3}$$

Direct substitution gives $\frac{8}{0^+}$. A positive number divided by an infinitesimally small positive number shoots to positive infinity!

Module 3: Radical Conjugation & Rationalization

Question 9: Basic Radical Conjugate

Evaluate the limit: $$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{x + 16} - 4}{x}$$

Multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate $\sqrt{x + 16} + 4$. The numerator simplifies to $(x + 16) - 16 = x$.

Question 10: Shifted Radical Conjugate

Evaluate the limit: $$\lim_{x \to 5} \frac{\sqrt{x - 1} - 2}{x - 5}$$

Multiply top and bottom by $\sqrt{x - 1} + 2$. The numerator becomes $(x - 1) - 4 = x - 5$, which cancels $(x - 5)$ in the denominator.

Question 11: Radical in Denominator

Evaluate the limit: $$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x}{\sqrt{x + 4} - 2}$$

Multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator: $\sqrt{x + 4} + 2$.

Question 12: Complex Conjugate Form

Evaluate the limit: $$\lim_{x \to 1} \frac{\sqrt{8 + x} - 3}{x - 1}$$

Conjugate is $\sqrt{8 + x} + 3$. Numerator simplifies to $(8 + x) - 9 = x - 1$.

Module 4: One-Sided Limits & The Rendezvous Criterion

Question 13: Absolute Value Left-Hand Limit

Evaluate the left-hand limit: $$\lim_{x \to 0^-} \frac{|x|}{x}$$

For $x < 0$, $|x| = -x$. Thus $\frac{-x}{x} = -1$.

Question 14: Rendezvous Criterion Failure

Given a piecewise function where $\lim_{x \to c^-} f(x) = 5$ and $\lim_{x \to c^+} f(x) = 8$, what can we conclude about $\lim_{x \to c} f(x)$?

Check the Rendezvous Criterion in Section 3. Do the bridge crews meet?

Question 15: Piecewise Function Limit

Let $f(x) = \begin{cases} x^2 + 1 & \text{if } x < 2 \\ 3x - 1 & \text{if } x \ge 2 \end{cases}$. What is $\lim_{x \to 2^-} f(x)$?

For $x < 2$, use the first sub-function $x^2 + 1$ and substitute $x = 2$.

Question 16: Two-Sided Piecewise Check

Using the same piecewise function from Question 15 ($f(x) = x^2+1$ for $x < 2$ and $3x-1$ for $x \ge 2$), does $\lim_{x \to 2} f(x)$ exist?

Calculate both $\lim_{x \to 2^-} (x^2+1) = 5$ and $\lim_{x \to 2^+} (3x-1) = 5$. Compare them!

Module 5: Limit Laws & The Squeeze Theorem

Question 17: Squeeze Theorem Application

If $3x \le f(x) \le x^3 + 2x$ for all $x$, what is $\lim_{x \to 1} f(x)$?

Evaluate the limit of the floor $3(1) = 3$ and ceiling $(1)^3 + 2(1) = 3$ as $x \to 1$.

Question 18: Landmark Trigonometric Limit

Evaluate the fundamental limit: $$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(x)}{x}$$

Proved via the Squeeze Theorem in Section 5 (trapped between $\cos(x)$ and $1$).

Question 19: Scaled Trig Limit

Evaluate the limit: $$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(5x)}{x}$$

Multiply top and bottom by $5$: $\frac{5\sin(5x)}{5x} = 5 \cdot \frac{\sin(5x)}{5x}$. As $5x \to 0$, this evaluates to $5 \cdot 1 = 5$.
