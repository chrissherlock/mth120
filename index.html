Welcome to the Journey! 📐

Mathematics is not a collection of disconnected recipes—it is a continuous landscape of ideas connecting the discrete to the smooth, the static to the dynamic, and individual equations to multidimensional spaces.

🎯 1. Sets, Sequences & Limits

Foundations of mathematical logic, number closures ($\mathbb{N} \subset \mathbb{Z} \subset \mathbb{Q} \subset \mathbb{R}$), and formal $\epsilon\text{–}N$ limit definitions.

⚡ 2. Functions, Continuity & Derivatives

Mapping behavior, $\epsilon\text{–}\delta$ continuity, rates of change, optimization, and concavity.

📊 3. Accumulation & Integration

Riemann sums, the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, areas between curves, and volumes of revolution.

🧩 4. Linear Algebra & Vectors

Systems of linear equations, Gaussian elimination, determinants, vector spaces, and complex numbers ($\mathbb{C}$).
